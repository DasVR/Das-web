# Portfolio Direction — Das Web Design

## Vibe
Dark editorial + cinematic (A24 meets bymonolog)

## References
- bymonolog.com — massive typography, dark, numbered sections, client stats
- mainframe.co.uk — slash sections (/About, /Work), editorial layout
- a24films.com — cinematic, moody, big imagery

## Key Elements
- Dark background (near-black, not pure black)
- Bold massive typography for headers
- Numbered sections (01, 02, 03)
- Client results with real numbers ($X, X% increase)
- Slash-style section labels (/Work, /About)
- Cinematic feel with subtle motion
- Clean project showcase grid
- Services list with clear hierarchy

## Sections
1. Hero — big name + tagline + CTA
2. Selected Work — 3-4 projects with images + results
3. Services — what you do (web design, etc)
4. About — short story + photo
5. Contact — email + phone + form

## Color Palette
- Background: #0a0a0a or #111
- Text: #fff / #e5e5e5
- Accent: subtle (maybe a warm tone or just white)
- No bright colors, keep it moody

## Typography
- Display: massive, bold, tight tracking
- Body: clean sans, readable
- Section labels: monospace or small caps
