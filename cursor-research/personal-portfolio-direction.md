# Personal + Portfolio Direction — Broad Small Business

Updated: 2026-07-24  
Status: **Active north star** (supersedes trade-only / Largo-only framing)

---

## Who this site is for now

**Arriq / Das Web Design** is a **personal website + portfolio + service offering**.

Not a niche “plumber websites only” shop. Not a faceless agency.

| Layer | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Personal** | First-person. Arriq’s taste, process, voice. About is real, not corp-speak. |
| **Portfolio** | Selected work, experiments, case studies when ready. Proof over promises. |
| **Services** | Hireable for small businesses & independents — many industries, not one vertical. |
| **Location** | Based in Largo / Tampa Bay area, **open to clients anywhere**. Soft-local, not locked-local. |

**Still true:** small business focus, approachable pricing, fast clean sites that convert.  
**No longer the headline:** trades-only, Largo-only, HVAC/plumber pitch as the whole brand.

---

## Positioning (one sentence)

> I’m Arriq — I design and build websites (and related brand/web work) for small businesses and independents who need to look legit and get real inquiries — from wherever you are.

Optional shorter:  
> Personal portfolio & web design for small businesses.

---

## Audience (broad SMB)

Keep “small business,” widen the verticals:

- Professional services (consultants, coaches, clinics, law/accounting light)
- Retail & e-commerce lite
- Restaurants / cafes / hospitality
- Creators, studios, freelancers
- Trades & home services *(still welcome — just not the only story)*
- Local brands expanding online
- Early startups needing a real presence without agency bloat

**Geography:** US-friendly / remote-capable. Mention home base once; don’t lead every line with Largo.

---

## Site jobs (in order)

1. **Feel like Arriq** — taste, motion, editorial dark aesthetic (keep custom cursor, DotMatrix, slash headers)
2. **Show work** — portfolio / selected stories
3. **Explain services** — clear menu of what you sell
4. **Make contact stupid easy** — email, phone, form
5. **Optionally deepen** later: writing, experiments, photography, case study pages

## Copy framework (use on site)

### Hero
- Brand: ARRIQ (DotMatrix)
- Headline: keep strong / editorial (“Websites that speak your brand’s voice.” or iterate)
- Sub: small businesses & independents · design + build · based in Florida, working widely
- CTAs: View Work · Start a Project

### About
- First person
- Taste + craft + who you help
- Home base mentioned lightly
- Not: “I only do plumbers in Largo”

### Work
- Mix of industries when available
- Metrics when real; “Coming Soon” ok
- Personal projects welcome next to client work

### Services
- Giant stacked names or clean rows
- Expand for detail + starting price
- Avoid trade-only language in SEO blurbs

### Contact
- “Based in Largo, FL — working with clients wherever you are.”

- Hit-list / cold email tone on the marketing site
- Service copy that only mentions plumbers/HVAC/Google Maps Largo
